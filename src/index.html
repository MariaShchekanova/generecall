    A second look, in three steps.

    01 · Bring your report

    Paste a report excerpt or upload a plain text (.txt) file. Use “Read my report” to extract its key details, then confirm or correct them. PDF upload is not supported.

    02 · Follow the evidence

    GeneRecall looks up the exact ClinVar identifier you confirm. It shows the record's aggregate classification, review status, date, and source. Different clinical contexts and submissions may explain a difference.

    03 · Start the conversation

    Review and edit a follow-up request for your original testing laboratory or a genetics professional. Copy, download, or print it.

    About the demo

    The demo report is synthetic. It is compared with a dated, sourced ClinVar snapshot. The old classification is illustrative and is not evidence of a real historical reclassification.

    About your data

    Report text is held in this page's memory and sent to this app's server when you extract or compare. When AI is configured, extraction also sends it to OpenAI for processing. No account or report history is stored by this app. Avoid personal identifying information.

    Your AI connection.

    Checking connection…

    Transparent by design

    When AI is configured and available, OpenAI extracts report details. The app builds your follow-up from a structured template. ClinVar remains the source for variant evidence. Every extracted field is yours to confirm.

    Without AI, the app uses a clearly labeled rules engine and a structured letter template. The demo is always available.

    Your data, this session

    Reports are not saved to a database or browser storage. Text you submit is processed by the app server. When configured, extraction also sends it to OpenAI. Remove personal identifiers before using real reports.
